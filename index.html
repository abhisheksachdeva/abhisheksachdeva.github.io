Places to explore

Firozpur

Explore the historic city of Firozpur, known for its rich cultural heritage and significant historical landmarks.

Must Visit:

• Gurudwara Jamni sahib - Associated with Guru Gobind Singh Ji's visit

• Saragarhi Memorial - A tribute to the 21 Sikh soldiers

• Hussainiwala Border - Witness the flag lowering ceremony

Amritsar

Visit the spiritual capital of Punjab, home to the Golden Temple and rich Sikh heritage.

Chandigarh

Experience the planned city designed by Le Corbusier, known for its modern architecture and beautiful gardens.
